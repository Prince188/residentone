## 1. Accounting ERP (~60 features)
Purpose-built RWA ERP, not generic accounting. Replaces Tally/Excel for societies.

**Billing Setup:**
- Multiple charge types (per sq ft, fixed, slab, consumption-based)
- Automated accounting system with ready-to-use templates
- Unlimited billing heads (water, parking, sinking fund etc)
- Group invoicing (multiple charges + different GST rates in one bill)
- Targeted invoicing (by tower, block, owner vs tenant, flat type)
- Custom invoice format (logo, header/footer, pagination)
- Custom invoicing sequence (prefixes, numbering)
- E-invoicing with dynamic UPI QR code
- Recurring invoices (auto monthly/quarterly)
- Automatic scheduled invoicing (date/period based)
- Auto charge calculation (by size, type, consumption)
- Bulk upload (invoices/credits)
- Customisation (GST toggle line-item level, item-wise credit/discount/GST)

**Penalties & Advances:**
- Interest and penalties (configurable rules, flat/%/step)
- Fine options (rate/amount/percentage/custom, slab fines, Mumbai fine system from invoice date)
- Flexible penalty setup + auto-reversal of fine if paid within grace
- Advance account (multiple advance types) + settlement from advance (auto)
- Arrears (single total or broken-down in new invoice)
- Automation for defaulters (flag overdue, reminders, penalties, restrict amenity access)

**Payments & Reconciliation:**
- Multiple app payment options (UPI, net banking, cards, VPA, foreign cards, RuPay/Diners/Amex)
- Least transaction charges (optimised)
- Partial payment options
- Credit notes & discounts (issue against past/upcoming invoices)
- Payment intimation (offline EFT/cheque/cash recorded)
- Auto reconciliation (online payments) + reconciliation exception report + cash payment reconciliation
- Auto bank reconciliation via custom fields in statement
- Multi-bank support (different banks for different bill types)
- Purchases flow (purchase requisition -> RFQ -> PO -> expense booking -> approval -> inventory receipt -> delivery tracking)
- Budget (monthly/quarterly/annual + real-time variance)

**Ledgers & Vouchers:**
- General ledger (assets/liabilities/income/expenses)
- General payments (petty cash small payments)
- General receipts (misc income)
- Chart of accounts (heads with hierarchy linking GL/category/sub-category)
- Journal vouchers (debit/credit adjustments) + editable/deletable + canceled review
- Fixed deposit tracker (interest, maturity, bank)
- Security deposit tracking (amenity/move-in)
- Cash and bank transfers (between office & banks)
- Uploads/downloads (bulk daily transactions for audit)
- Accounts migration (from existing system)
- Easy Tally export (sync, import invoices/receipts)

**Residents View:**
- App dues statement (dues + overdue)
- App account statement (invoices/receipts/dues/deposits/advances viewable & payable)
- Auto dues receipts (online/advance)
- Prepaid meters integration (recharge electricity/water via app, 14+ brands: El-Measure, Secure, WaterOn, Radius, WeGot etc) + real-time usage + failure logs & retry
- Offline intimation via app/email/SMS
- Reminders (push/SMS/email for pending)
- Dues reports (society-wide, bill-specific, head-wise), collection reports (flat/bill/head-wise), advance collection report, dues+advance+security deposit report (period filter), income & expense reports with charts, TDS report, GST report (input/output), trial balance (accrued income/provisions), balance sheet (Schedule VI & N), day book (all payments/receipts/vouchers per day), defaulters list

**Additional ERP:**
- Schedule reports (auto email)
- Expense booking with GST/TDS + admin approval + download reports (paid/unpaid vendor)
- Audit-ready trails + detailed audit logs (who approved/edited/sent invoice) + MIS/SLA/TAT summaries + performance tracking (manager activities across accounting)
